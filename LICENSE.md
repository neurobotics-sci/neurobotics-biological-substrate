They build the bodies, we build the brains. To protect the integrity of our research from corporate enclosure while simultaneously providing our Enterprise and Defense partners with the secure, proprietary environments they require, Neurobotics operates under a strict Dual-Licensing Model.

1. THE COMMUNITY TIER (OPEN SOURCE)

License: [GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 (AGPL-3.0)]

Our core computational substrate and bare-metal mathematical frameworks are available to the academic and open-source communities under the AGPL 3.0 license.

The terms are simple and strictly enforced:

    • Free Use & Modification: You are free to use, modify, and distribute the code for research, academic, and non-commercial purposes.
    • The "Copyleft" Clause: If you modify this software and make it available over a network, you must release your complete modified source code back to the community under the same AGPL-3.0 license.
    • Purpose: This license acts as a definitive shield against unauthorized proprietary enclosure. It ensures that any entity attempting to privatize our foundational science without a commercial agreement is legally compelled to open-source their entire integrated stack.

2. THE ENTERPRISE & DEFENSE TIER (COMMERCIAL LICENSE)

License: Master Commercial License Agreement (MCLA)

For Venture Capital partners, Defense Contractors (DoD, AFWERX, SOCOM), and Corporate Integrators who require closed-source modifications and deployment into their own physical hardware, Neurobotics offers a proprietary Commercial License.

The Commercial License provides:
    • Exemption from AGPL-3.0: Completely removes the viral copyleft restrictions, allowing your organization to integrate the Neurobotics cognitive substrate into highly classified, proprietary, or closed-source chassis systems without exposing your source code.
    • Audit & Compliance Readiness: Dedicated support for FIPS 140-3 and DISA-STIG compliance pipelines, ensuring our bare-metal architecture meets strict military and aerospace regulatory standards.
    • IP Protection: Safely builds your proprietary applications and robotic hardware on top of the Neurobotics foundation while respecting the core Renshaw Theory intellectual property.
To initiate a Master Commercial License Agreement, please contact Shannon Renshaw, Chief Operating & Communications Officer (COO/CCO).

3. ARCHITECTURE INTEGRATION & EVALUATION PILOTS

License: Evaluation Integration Agreement (EIA)

Because Neurobotics licenses the cognitive architecture (the "brains") rather than manufacturing the physical robotics (the "bodies"), pilot deployments are governed by an Evaluation Integration Agreement. Our physical chassis (Mark XIV "Bubo") serves strictly as a mathematical proof-of-concept. Our deliverable is the deterministic code and firmware, designed to scale down to 1-3 edge-compute boards utilizing less power than a standard LED bulb.

This agreement covers:

    • Architecture Porting: Defined operational windows for integrating the Neurobotics cognitive matrix into your proprietary chassis or regional federal validation labs.
    • IP & Reverse-Engineering Prohibitions: Strict protections safeguarding our bare-metal code, mathematics, and proprietary firmware during the evaluation phase.
    • Hardware Agnosticism: Terms governing the deployment of our code onto your physical assets, ensuring our software operates securely and efficiently on your designated substrates.

4. CONTRIBUTING TO NEUROBOTICS

Requirement: Contributor License Agreement (CLA)

We welcome brilliant minds who wish to contribute to the deterministic AI revolution. To accept any external pull requests or code contributions into the core Neurobotics repositories, contributors must sign our Contributor License Agreement (CLA).
    • Why? This legally transfers the copyright of the contributed code to Neurobotics, ensuring we maintain the clean IP lineage required to offer our Commercial and Defense tiers without encumbrance.
