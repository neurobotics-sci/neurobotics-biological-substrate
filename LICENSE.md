## OUR PHILOSOPHY: ESSE QUAM VIDERI
At Neurobotics, we believe in the physical, deterministic execution of artificial intelligence. We are dedicated to advancing the foundational science of Vector Symbolic Architectures (VSA) and bare-metal hyperdimensional computing. 

To protect the integrity of our research from corporate enclosure while simultaneously providing our Enterprise and Defense partners with the secure, proprietary environments they require, Neurobotics operates under a strict **Dual-Licensing Model**.

---

## 1. THE COMMUNITY TIER (OPEN SOURCE)
**License:** [GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 (AGPL-3.0)](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/agpl-3.0.html)

Our core computational substrate and bare-metal mathematical frameworks are available to the academic and open-source communities under the AGPL-3.0 license. 

**The terms are simple and strictly enforced:**
* **Free Use & Modification:** You are free to use, modify, and distribute the code for research, academic, and non-commercial purposes.
* **The "Copyleft" Clause:** If you modify this software and make it available over a network, you **must** release your complete modified source code back to the community under the same AGPL-3.0 license. 
* **Purpose:** This license acts as a definitive shield against unauthorized proprietary enclosure. It ensures that any entity attempting to privatize our foundational science without a commercial agreement is legally compelled to open-source their entire integrated stack.

---

## 2. THE ENTERPRISE & DEFENSE TIER (COMMERCIAL LICENSE)
**License:** Master Commercial License Agreement (MCLA)

For Venture Capital partners, Defense Contractors (DoD, AFWERX, SOCOM), and Corporate Integrators who require closed-source modifications and physical hardware deployments, Neurobotics offers a proprietary Commercial License.

**The Commercial License provides:**
* **Exemption from AGPL-3.0:** Completely removes the viral copyleft restrictions, allowing your organization to integrate the S-BUD chassis and our VSA substrate into highly classified, proprietary, or closed-source systems without exposing your source code.
* **Audit & Compliance Readiness:** Dedicated support for FIPS 140-3 and DISA STIG compliance pipelines, ensuring our bare-metal architecture meets strict military and aerospace regulatory standards.
* **IP Protection:** Safely builds your proprietary applications on top of the Neurobotics foundation while respecting the core Renshaw Theory intellectual property.

*To initiate a Master Commercial License Agreement, please contact Shannon Renshaw, Chief Operating & Communications Officer (COO/CCO).*

---

## 3. HARDWARE PILOT & EVALUATION DEPLOYMENTS
**License:** Hardware Evaluation Agreement (HEA)

Because Neurobotics provides physical, bare-metal edge compute chassis (S-BUD), all physical pilot deployments are governed by a Hardware Evaluation Agreement. 

**This agreement covers:**
* **Chain of Custody:** Strict physical security and tracking of the <15W RISC-V compute chassis.
* **Reverse-Engineering Prohibitions:** Hardware tamper protections and IP safeguarding during the evaluation phase.
* **Pilot Timelines:** Defined operational windows for integration testing at facilities such as regional commercial deployment hubs or federal validation labs.

---

## 4. CONTRIBUTING TO NEUROBOTICS
**Requirement:** Contributor License Agreement (CLA)

We welcome brilliant minds who wish to contribute to the deterministic AI revolution. To accept any external pull requests or code contributions into the core Neurobotics repositories, contributors must sign our Contributor License Agreement (CLA). 

* **Why?** This legally transfers the copyright of the contributed code to Neurobotics, ensuring we maintain the clean IP lineage required to offer our Commercial and Defense tiers without encumbrance.
